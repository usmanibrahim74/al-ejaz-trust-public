About Our Foundation

Our Mission & Vision

Founded in 2015, Al Ejaz Trust International was born from a deep commitment to serve humanity with compassion and dignity. We believe that every individual, regardless of their circumstances, deserves access to basic necessities, education, and healthcare.

Our foundation focuses on supporting orphaned children, widowed women, and economically disadvantaged families across rural and urban communities. Through sustainable programs and transparent operations, we aim to break the cycle of poverty and create pathways to self-sufficiency.

With the unwavering support of our donors and volunteers, we've touched over 15,000 lives and raised more than £2.5 million to fund critical initiatives. Every penny donated is accounted for and directed toward maximum impact.

Sabeel in Muharram

Rooh Afza & Jam-e-Sheerin • Serving the Thirsty

During the scorching heat of Muharram, we set up a Sabeel — a traditional water station — offering refreshing Rooh Afza and nourishing Jam-e-Sheerin milk to all who passed by. This act of service honours the sacrifices of Imam Hussain (R.A) and his companions by quenching the thirst of travelers and community members, embodying the spirit of generosity and compassion that defines this sacred month.

Qurbani Project 2026

Fresh Meat Distributed to the Needy

For Qurbani 2026, we carried out the sacrifice and distributed fresh meat to poor and deserving families in our community. From the preparation to the final distribution, every step was handled with care and devotion, ensuring that families who rarely have meat on their tables could share in the blessings of Eid. This project is a continuation of our commitment to feeding the hungry and honouring the spirit of sacrifice.
